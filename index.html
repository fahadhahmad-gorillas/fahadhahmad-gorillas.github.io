🎯 What Is This Software?

Imagine you could see how a quantum particle (like an electron) travels through space and time. Our software does exactly that! It takes a quantum wave packet - the mathematical description of a particle's position and momentum - and shows you exactly how it moves, spreads, and interacts with its environment. The Split-Step Fourier Method is a sophisticated mathematical technique that makes these complex calculations fast and accurate.

Think of it as a weather forecast, but for quantum particles instead of storms! You see the full picture of how your quantum system behaves without being a math genius.

🔬 Technical Overview

For those interested in the physics and math behind this simulator, here's a brief glimpse:

The core equation being solved is the time-dependent Schrödinger equation:

iℏ ∂ψ/∂t = -ℏ²/2m ∇²ψ + V(x)ψ

Our simulator uses the Split-Step Fourier Method to evolve the wave function ψ(x,t) through time. This elegant mathematical trick splits the evolution into two separate steps - kinetic and potential - and solves each in the domain where it's most efficient (momentum space for kinetic, position space for potential). This gives us exceptional speed and accuracy.
